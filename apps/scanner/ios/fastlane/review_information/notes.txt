Technikpool Scanner is the handheld companion to Technikpool, a rental-inventory
system for event-production companies. In normal use it pairs with a customer's
own server, so there is no shared account to sign in with.

To review it without a server, tap "Explore the demo" at the bottom of the first
screen. That runs a small sample warehouse entirely on the device — no network
request is made, and nothing is stored beyond the app.

In the demo:
  * Scans — pick a location or production, then type an asset tag where it says
    "Manual entry". Tags run 40000001 to 40000012. On a device with a camera the
    camera button works too, but there is nothing to point it at.
  * Lookup — type the same tags to see an asset, where it is and its history.
  * Inventory — lists every asset with its tag, so nothing has to be guessed.

Leave the demo under Settings › Leave demo.

Camera access is requested only for reading barcodes, and only when the scan
button is used. The app runs on phones and on rugged warehouse handhelds that
have a physical barcode trigger instead of a camera.

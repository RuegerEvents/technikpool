• Print case labels: for a kit or a unit with accessories, a label with name, contents, logo and a scannable code on a ZPL label printer (e.g. Honeywell PC42E-T) on the warehouse network. Printer address under Settings.
• Kits in the app: view them in Inventory and edit with the pencil – scan units in (whole kits too), take them out, add loose cables by count. New kits and new devices (with a new product as well, with or without a label) from “+”.
• Inventory like the web: Bundle View, All Products, All Devices, with a forgiving search. Lookup now lives in Inventory: a scan or a typed number opens the unit or kit.
• Hand-out list: hand out by name and count, pack into pack cases and print per-production case labels. When a scanned unit or kit swaps places with the booked one, the scan says so.
• Safe from slips: what you look at opens to look at – changes go through the pencil or the ⋮ menu.

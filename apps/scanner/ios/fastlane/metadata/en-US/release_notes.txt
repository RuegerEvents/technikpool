• Check a production's equipment against its list: tap a production under Scans › Production and choose Check. Scan or tick what is there, section by section, together with others checking at the same time; nothing changes on the devices.
• Lent equipment: the lending organization checks its units out to the other production, and from a check the production confirms receiving them and later reports them sent back.
• Tapping a production asks whether to check out to it or check it.
